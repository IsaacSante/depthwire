# Run a show

What to sort out before the doors open.

## Wi-Fi or cable

| | Wi-Fi | Cable (Ethernet) |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Nothing to do | Two adapters and a cable, see [the gear list](gear.md) |
| Good for | Testing, short pieces | Anything running for hours |
| Risk | Busy venue Wi-Fi drops frames | A cable people can trip on: tape it down |

Both apps show which one is in use. On the Mac it's in the banner at the top; on the phone it's in the status card.

## Keep the phone alive

- **Power.** Use a phone-side adapter with a charging port, and plug the charger into it. Streaming depth for hours will drain the battery otherwise.
- **Screen.** DepthWire keeps the screen awake while it's sending. Leave the app open and in front.
- **Heat.** A phone that gets hot slows itself down. Keep it out of direct sun and out of a case if it runs warm.
- **Notifications.** Turn on a Focus mode so a call or an alert doesn't interrupt.

## Before the doors open

1. Banner on the Mac is green: "Receiving depth".
2. Calibrate with the room empty and the lights as they'll be during the show.
3. Walk the whole active area and watch your visuals respond at the edges.
4. Leave it running for an hour as a test, with the phone charging.

## If it stops

- **Banner goes red:** check the phone. Is DepthWire still open? Is the cable still in? It reconnects by itself once the phone is sending again.
- **Visuals react to nothing, or to everything:** the phone moved. Calibrate again.
- **The camera disappears from your app:** quit and reopen DepthWire on the Mac.
